Frequently Asked Questions

Direct answers for humans & search engines.

Clear answers on how custom web development, background AI automation, and Answer-Engine Optimization save hours and drive business growth.

How much does custom web design and AI automation cost in Sacramento?

Custom web development and AI automation projects at Capitol CoDesign are delivered under binding, fixed-scope proposals with no surprise fees. Every proposal is written after a focused 45-minute discovery audit. Clients receive 100% full ownership of all code, assets, and accounts across Sacramento, Roseville, Folsom, El Dorado Hills, and Northern California.

How does Answer-Engine Optimization (AEO) get my business recommended by ChatGPT and Perplexity?

Answer-Engine Optimization (AEO) formats your website content and data using structured JSON-LD schema, concise direct answer blocks, and high-density entity markup. When prospective clients ask conversational queries in AI assistants like ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini, or Perplexity, AEO ensures these AI search engines index, cite, and recommend your company as the top local authority.

Who owns the code, content, and accounts after project launch?

You own 100% of your source code, website content, domain names, analytics, and advertising accounts outright. Capitol CoDesign never locks you into proprietary platforms, hidden fees, or recurring retainers unless you explicitly request ongoing monthly support.

Can AI automations integrate with my existing CRM and business software?

Yes, our custom AI agents and workflow engines connect directly into the tools you already pay for. We integrate with HubSpot, Salesforce, Zapier, Google Workspace, Resend API, and custom REST APIs to automate client intake, scheduling, document drafting, and customer support.

Why hire a local Sacramento engineering agency over remote freelancers?

Working with a local Sacramento agency provides 30+ years of production technology experience with direct 1:1 access to the principal engineer. You get local accountability, fixed-scope proposals, and same-day response times across Sacramento, Roseville, Folsom, El Dorado Hills, Davis, and the Bay Area.

How do you protect website forms from bot submissions and spam?

We deploy multi-layered form protection including invisible Cloudflare Turnstile token verification, client timing checks, honeypot traps, and serverless backend validation. Form submissions pass through Netlify Functions and Resend API to deliver clean inquiries without annoying human visitors with visual CAPTCHAs.
