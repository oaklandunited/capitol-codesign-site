Frequently Asked Questions

Direct answers for humans & search engines.

Clear answers on how custom web development, background AI automation, and Answer-Engine Optimization save hours and drive business growth.

What is practical AI automation for small businesses?

Practical AI automation replaces repetitive manual tasks—such as client intake, lead qualification, appointment scheduling, and invoicing—with background workflow agents that save staff hours, rather than decorative homepage chatbots.

How does Answer-Engine Optimization (AEO) work?

AEO structures your website data, schemas, and content so AI search assistants like ChatGPT, Claude, and Gemini can index, cite, and recommend your business when prospective clients ask conversational queries.

Why hire Capitol CoDesign instead of a traditional digital agency?

Capitol CoDesign offers 30+ years of engineering experience with direct principal access, fixed-scope proposals, transparent pricing, and 100% full ownership of code and assets.
