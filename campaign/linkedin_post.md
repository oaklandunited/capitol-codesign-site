## Strategy Details & Formatting

- **The Hook**: Lines 1 & 2 target a common frustration (useless chatbots) to instantly capture attention and avoid truncation on mobile feeds.
- **Tone**: Pragmatic, direct, and senior. It contrasts "flashy AI" with "work-reducing AI," which builds high trust with business operators.
- **Whitespace**: Single-sentence paragraphs and clean emoji lists make it readable for busy professionals scrolling on mobile.
