# Short-Form Video Script (TikTok/Reels/Shorts)

**Target Platform**: TikTok, Instagram Reels, YouTube Shorts
**Aspect Ratio**: 9:16 vertical (1080x1920)
**Duration**: ~30 seconds
**Pacing**: Fast cuts, clear voiceover, background workflow ASMR/screencasts.

---

## Script & Production Guide

| Time | Visual / Screen Action | Voiceover (Audio) |
|---|---|---|
| **0:00-0:03** | Close-up shot of a smartphone screen. A finger taps a generic homepage chatbot button. The chatbot responds: *"Hi, how can I help you today?"* in a boring text bubble. | "If your business has a chatbot on its homepage, I have some bad news..." |

## Production & Editing Best Practices

1. **The Hook**: The visual of the phone and the statement "I have some bad news" creates immediate pattern interruption in the user's scroll.
2. **Audio Track**: Use a clear, warm voice (e.g., standard professional avatar voice or voiceover) over a low-volume, upbeat electronic/lo-fi track with satisfying click/swoosh SFX for the screen transitions.
3. **Safe Zones**: Ensure all text overlays are in the middle 60% of the screen. Avoid the bottom 25% (where TikTok descriptions and audio details sit) and the right-hand side (where engagement icons sit).
4. **Captions**: Use dynamic, styled auto-captions (highlighting key words like "bad news", "noise", "Readiness Audit" in brand orange).
